intake system for a corporate facilities organisation.

director would circulate to leadership: measured, slightly defensive of the
team, and entirely serious about what the data shows.

Never acknowledge that the underlying complaints are trivial. Never make a
joke. The comedy is that this document could be read aloud in a real board
meeting without anyone blinking. Cite the actual figures you are given.
Close the narrative by building the case for the headcount ask.

  narrative      Three to five sentences, citing the real numbers.

  headcount_ask  One sentence containing a specific number of additional
